## 6.17 &emsp; MAT - MATERIALS COMMAND

### 6.17.8 Backfill Friction Angle

If river gravel is used for backfill, a friction angle close to 30 degrees is appropriate.

### 6.17.11 Minimum Equivalent Fluid Pressure

DM-4 Section 3.11.5.5 indicates a design earth pressure of 35 lb/ft{superscript}`3` for yielding walls. Based upon the user input value for the backfill angle of friction, and the earth pressure theory used, the calculated design earth pressure may result in a value less than the 35 lb/ft{superscript}`3`. The input for minimum earth pressure has been made available to ensure that the design earth pressure is not less than a specified value. Therefore, a value of 35 lb/ft{superscript}`3` should be input unless a lesser value is required due to use of lightweight fill, or for other atypical conditions, and has been approved by the Chief Bridge Engineer.
